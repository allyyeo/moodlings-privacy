Moodlings Privacy Policy

From Moodlings Studio. Effective 20 October 2026.

The short version: Moodlings has no account. What you write and feel stays on your phone. We do not run ads, tracking or analytics, and we never sell data.

What stays on your phone

Moodlings keeps these on your device only (in the app's local storage): your photos in Photo magic, your visits, your feelings ratings, your diary notes, your Joy Jar moments, your badges, your settings, and simple counts of which features you use. We never receive them and never send them anywhere. If Android backup is on, your phone may back up this app data to your own Google account so it can come back on a new phone; that backup belongs to you and we never see it.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also delete visits in Past visits and diary notes in the diary at any time.

Photos

In Photo magic you can add pictures from your phone using Android's photo picker, which only shares the pictures you choose. Copies are kept inside the app on your phone, are never uploaded or shared, and are deleted when you delete them in the app or remove the app. Backup files you make in Settings do not include photos.

No account

You do not sign up or log in. We do not ask for your name, email, location or contacts.

Reminders and motion

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your phone. It does not go through our servers. Some activities use the motion sensor so you can shake your phone. The sensor is read on your device only and nothing is recorded or sent.

Purchases

Moodlings Pro purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not see your payment details. To know whether Moodlings Pro is active, we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase records. Read their policy here: RevenueCat Privacy Policy. A lifetime plan stays active for as long as Moodlings is offered on Google Play.

Need support? link

The "Need support?" button opens findahelpline.com in your browser. That is a third-party site with its own privacy policy. We do not send it anything about you.

What we do not do

  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • No analytics
  • No selling or sharing your data

Children

Moodlings is designed for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and the app has no account to collect it with.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will say so in the app.
