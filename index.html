Moodlings Privacy Policy

From Moodlings Studio. Last updated: 21 October 2026.

The short version: Moodlings has no account. What you write and feel stays on your phone. We do not run ads, tracking or analytics, and we never sell data.

What stays on your phone

Moodlings keeps these on your device only, in the app's own storage:

  • your visits: the monster you picked, your 1 to 10 ratings before and after, and the feeling words you chose
  • the short notes you write with the magic pencil (what, how and why)
  • your Diary notes and the sparkles you save
  • the suns in your Joy Jar and the good things you told Sunny
  • your badges
  • your photos in Photo magic
  • your settings: language, room colour, music, sound effects, tips, reminder time
  • simple counts of which games you try, used only inside the app

We never receive any of this and never send it anywhere. If Android backup is on, your phone may back up this app data to your own Google account so it can come back on a new phone. That backup belongs to you and we never see it.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also delete visits in the Mood log, let Diary notes float away, empty your Joy Jars and reset your badges at any time.

Photos

In Photo magic you can add pictures from your phone using Android's photo picker, which only shares the pictures you choose. Moodlings does not use your camera. Copies are kept inside the app on your phone, are never uploaded or shared, and are deleted when you delete them in the app or remove the app.

Backup files

In Settings you can save a backup file to move to a new phone. You choose where it goes, using your phone's share menu. It includes your visits, notes, jars, badges and settings, but not your photos or your plan. We never receive it. Keep it somewhere private.

No account

You do not sign up or log in. We do not ask for your name, email, age, location or contacts.

Reminders and motion

If you turn on the daily Diary reminder, Android asks you first, and the notification is scheduled on your phone. It does not go through our servers. Some games use the motion sensor so you can shake your phone. The sensor is read on your device only, and nothing is recorded or sent. Moodlings does not use your microphone.

Purchases

Moodlings Pro purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not see your payment details. To know whether Moodlings Pro is active, we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase records from Google Play. Read their policy here: RevenueCat Privacy Policy. A lifetime plan stays active for as long as Moodlings is offered on Google Play.

If you use a gift code or manage your subscription, Moodlings opens the Google Play page for it. Google's privacy policy applies there.

What we do not do

  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • No analytics
  • No selling or sharing your data

Not a medical tool

Moodlings is a mood companion. It is not a medical tool, therapy or a crisis service. If you feel unsafe, please reach out to someone you trust or a professional near you.

Children

Moodlings is made for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and the app has no account to collect it with.

Languages

The app is available in English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. If a translation of this policy ever differs from this English page, this page applies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will say so in the app.
